Privacy Policy — Calette Studio Automation

Effective date: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Calette Studio (the developer) handles information in connection with the Calette Studio Automation application ("the App") that connects to the Pinterest API.

What the App does

The App is a personal automation tool used solely by its developer, Calette Studio, to:

  • Create and manage Pins and Boards on the developer's own Pinterest account
  • Read analytics and performance data about the developer's own Pins and Boards
  • Schedule and publish content featuring Calette Studio's own digital products

What data the App accesses

  • Pinterest account data: With explicit authorization granted via Pinterest's OAuth login flow, the App accesses only the authenticated developer's own Pins, Boards, and related account data. The App does not access, collect, or process data belonging to any other Pinterest user or "Pinner."
  • Authorization tokens: The App stores OAuth access tokens provided by Pinterest to authenticate requests on the developer's own account. Tokens are stored securely in local configuration files and are never shared, published, or transferred to third parties.
  • Content created by the developer: Product images, titles, descriptions, and links for Calette Studio's own digital products.

What the App does NOT do

  • Does not collect, store, or share personal data from other users
  • Does not sell, rent, or trade any data
  • Does not use data for advertising profiling
  • Does not make automated decisions about any individual
  • Does not access Pins or Boards other than those of the authorized developer account

Data storage and security

  • Data and tokens are stored locally on the developer's own infrastructure in access-controlled configuration
  • Access tokens are used exclusively to perform the actions described above on the developer's own account
  • The developer follows reasonable technical safeguards to protect stored credentials

Third-party services

The App interacts with the Pinterest API under Pinterest's Developer Guidelines and API Terms. Please also review Pinterest's own Privacy Policy: policy.pinterest.com/privacy-policy
